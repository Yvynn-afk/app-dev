## About **The Notebook***

**The Notebook** is a romantic drama film released in 2004, directed by Nick Cassavetes and based on the novel written by Nicholas Sparks. The movie stars Ryan Gosling as Noah Calhoun and Rachel McAdams as Allie Hamilton.

The story follows two people who fall deeply in love despite coming from different social backgrounds. Noah is a simple young man, while Allie comes from a wealthy family. Their relationship faces many challenges, including family disagreements, distance, misunderstandings, and the passage of time.

## About **How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days

**How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days** is a romantic comedy film released in 2003, directed by Donald Petrie. The movie stars Kate Hudson as Andie Anderson and Matthew McConaughey as Benjamin Barry.

The story follows two people who start dating because of separate challenges. Andie wants to write an article about how to make a man leave a woman within ten days. Meanwhile, Ben makes a bet that he can make any woman fall in love with him within the same amount of time. Neither of them knows the other's real intentions.

## About *The Girl Next Door**

**The Girl Next Door** is a romantic comedy film released in 2004, directed by Luke Greenfield. The movie stars Emile Hirsch as Matthew Kidman and Elisha Cuthbert as Danielle.

The story follows Matthew, a high school student who has always focused on his studies and future. His life changes when a beautiful and friendly girl named Danielle moves into the house next door. As they spend more time together, Matthew begins to fall in love with her. However, he discovers something unexpected about Danielle's past that makes their relationship more complicated.
